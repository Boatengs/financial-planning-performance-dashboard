## Classes

- **Reported**: present in an official source field (SEC filing, T-100, Form 41).
- **Derived**: calculated only from reported values with the formula shown. SEC quarters obtained as
  full year minus nine months, or as a year-to-date difference, are stored as reported metrics with
  the method recorded in `fact_financial_actual.method`.
- **Modeled**: depends on the analyst FP&A model (budget, forecast, scenarios). Not company guidance.
